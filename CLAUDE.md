# experiment-framework

A multi-team repository audit framework (4 teams × 6 Python packages).

- **Setting up or operating the experiment?** Follow [RUNME.md](RUNME.md) step by step: `bash setup.sh`, then `./launch.sh`. Background is in [README.md](README.md).
- **Running the audit?** Your instructions are the pasted [KICKOFF_PROMPT.md](KICKOFF_PROMPT.md). Stay within its scope.
- **Never read `grading/`.** It holds expected outcomes and exists only for post-run analysis.
- Don't edit anything under `repos/`. Those are the audit targets.
